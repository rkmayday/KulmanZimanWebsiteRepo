01 · Why metallic fuel

More out of every reactor, starting at the core.

Vulcaris fuel systems are designed around four goals. Each one changes what an advanced reactor can do.

01

Longer operating lifetimes

Fuel that stays in the reactor longer means fewer refuelling stops and more time making power.

02

Higher power density

More energy from the same space, so reactors can be smaller for the power they deliver.

03

Better thermal performance

Metallic fuel moves heat out quickly, keeping the core cooler and the margins wider.

04

Greater reactor efficiency

Better fuel lets the whole plant run closer to its potential.

02 · Applications

One fuel platform. Many reactors.

Rather than building around a single reactor design, Vulcaris is developing fuel that can serve advanced reactor developers across every market.

Terrestrial energy

Clean, steady power for the grid, from compact advanced reactors that run longer between refuelling.

Defense

Resilient, long-lasting power for bases and remote sites that cannot depend on a fuel supply line.

Industrial

High-temperature heat and power for industry, from chemicals to manufacturing, without the emissions.

Space

Compact, dense power for missions and surface operations far from the sun.
