Reviewed against the live site on 2026-11-06 (Home, Products, About, Affiliates, Help, Legal).

## Fixed in this rebuild

| # | Problem on the live site | What the rebuild does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Most of the homepage is blank until you scroll.** Sections start invisible and only fade in when a scroll animation runs. In a full-page capture, everything below the mission quote is empty, so link previews, some crawlers, and slow or old phones can see a blank page. | Content is visible by default. The fade-in only applies when JavaScript is running, and a 2.5 s fallback shows everything regardless. Respects "reduce motion". |

## Recommended next (needs you, not code)

1. **Get a domain email** such as `hello@kulmanziman.shop` instead of three @gmail.com addresses. This is the single biggest trust upgrade for a $300 purchase, and it costs a few dollars a month (Google Workspace, Zoho, or forwarding through your registrar).
2. **Real founder photos.** Faces convert better than initials.
3. **First real testimonial.** After your first delivery, ask for a one-line quote and permission to show the site. Until then the Ironpath sample is your proof. Don't add made-up reviews.
4. **Offer hosting as an add-on** (for example, a yearly hosting and updates plan). Many small businesses can't deploy code themselves, and "you get code, hosting is on you" will lose sales.
5. **Whop checkout:** add a required "project details" field or post-purchase form in Whop. Then the "contact us after you pay" step can't be skipped.
6. Add **Google Search Console** and an analytics tool (Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics are privacy-friendly) so you can see what converts.
