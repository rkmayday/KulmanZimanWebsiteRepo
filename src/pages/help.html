Help

Questions, answered.

Pricing, process, timelines and refunds in one place.

Pricing & OrderingHow much does a website cost?
There are three packages, each paid once: Starter is $284 for a 1-page interactive website, Professional is $568 for 2–3 pages and Premium is $994 for 4–7 pages. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription, so there are no monthly fees. See the product →
Pricing & OrderingHow do I order?
Select Buy Now on the Products & Pricing page to open checkout, pay once, and your order starts. You can also check out from the homepage or the About & Contact page.
Pricing & OrderingWhat's included in each package?
A modern, mobile-friendly website tailored to your industry, plus all three stages of our refinement process: initial concept, second iteration and final delivery. After you order, we also register your domain. We pay for the first year if the registrar’s cost is below $30.00 USD, and we quote you before anything is purchased.
Pricing & OrderingShould I contact you before I pay?
Yes, please. Contact us before you pay, or immediately after you pay, at info.kulmanziman@gmail.com so we can confirm your specifications and discuss your domain. Send a brief →
Pricing & OrderingDo you maintain the website after delivery?
No. We deliver the website code and register your domain, paying for its first year when the registrar’s cost is below $30.00 USD. We do not host, maintain or update the website after final delivery. Read the Terms of Service →
Pricing & OrderingDoes the price include a domain name?
The package price pays for the website code. After you purchase, we register your domain once you approve our quote. If the first year costs less than $30.00 USD, we pay for it. If it is $30.00 USD or more, we charge a 20% markup on the registrar’s cost, as set out in your quote. Domain fees are generally non-refundable once registered. Read the Terms of Service →
Pricing & OrderingWhat happens after the first year?
Our domain coverage applies to the first year only. After that, renewing the domain and paying for hosting and maintenance is your responsibility. On request, we will provide what you need to transfer the domain to an account you control.
Pricing & OrderingWhat would a website like this one cost?
This website, the official Kulman Ziman site, has 6 pages: Home, Products & Pricing, About & Contact, Affiliate Program, Help and Legal. A 6-page site fits our Premium Website (4–7 pages), which is $994, paid once. Our four sample sites are one-page sites, so each of those would be a Starter Website at $284. See the packages →
Pricing & OrderingCan you build something bigger than the packages?
Yes. If you need more than seven pages, or a custom feature such as bookings or a member area, email info.kulmanziman@gmail.com and tell us what you need. We will quote it.
Process & TimelineWhat are the three stages of the refinement process?
Initial concept: a first draft based on your specifications. Second iteration: a refined version built from your feedback. Final delivery: the polished, completed project after your final round of revisions. View the process →
Process & TimelineHow long does it take?
We commit to completing the refinement process within one month of your order date, as long as you respond to each feedback request within 72 hours.
Process & TimelineWhat do you need from me?
Your specifications at the start, then your feedback or approval within 72 hours at each stage. Quick replies keep your project on schedule.
RefundsCan I get a refund?
Yes. If you respond within 72 hours at every stage and we still fail to complete the refinement process within one month, you are entitled to a full refund. Read the refund policy →
RefundsWhat happens if I don't respond quickly?
If a feedback request goes unanswered for more than 72 hours, the one-month window is extended by the length of your delay, and a refund does not apply to a delay caused by non-response.
RefundsHow do I request a refund?
Eligible refund requests are submitted and processed through Whop, and remain subject to Whop's own refund and buyer-protection policies.
About & ContactWho is behind Kulman Ziman?
Kulman Ziman was founded on August 24, 2026 by Rex Kulman and Ryder Ziman. Meet the founders →
About & ContactHow do I reach you?
Email info.kulmanziman@gmail.com. You can also email Ryder at ryderziman@gmail.com or Rex at rexkulman@gmail.com.
AffiliatesDo you have an affiliate program?
Yes. Affiliates earn 20% of every qualifying purchase made through their unique link. You bring us customers, and we build and deliver the website. See the affiliate program →
AffiliatesHow do I become an affiliate?
Sign up for our affiliate program on Whop at whop.com/kulman-ziman/affiliates. There is no application: you get your own affiliate link straight away and earn 20% on qualifying purchases.
LegalIs my payment information safe?
All payments are processed through Whop. Kulman Ziman does not collect or store your payment information directly. Privacy →
LegalWhat are your terms of service?
Products are sold and fulfilled through Whop, and purchases are subject to Whop's terms of service and buyer protections, plus any terms we provide at the time of sale. Terms of Service →
