FAQ

Good to know.

Do you have dairy-free milk?

Oat, almond and whole milk are all the same price.

Can I buy beans to take home?

Every coffee on the menu is available in 250 g and 1 kg bags, ground or whole bean.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free and fast. Laptops welcome at the long table.

Do you cater events?

We bring a mobile espresso bar and barista to events of 10 to 80 people. Book two weeks ahead.

Can I bring my own cup?

Please do. Bring your own cup and take 25 cents off.
