    Corner of Orchard and Pine. Bike racks out front, dogs welcome on the patio.

    FAQ

    Good to know.

    Do you have dairy-free milk?

    Yes: oat and almond, at no extra charge on Tuesdays.

    Can I buy beans to take home?

    Yes, whole or ground, in 250 g and 1 kg bags.

    Is there wifi and power?

    Both, at the long table by the window.

    Do you cater events?

    Yes, for groups of 10 to 80. Ask at the counter.
