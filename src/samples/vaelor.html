A dive watch has one job: keep the sea out and keep time. Ours does both, and every part that does it was finished by hand.

01

Tested to 200 metres

A screw-down crown and caseback, and every watch is pressure-tested in water before it leaves the workshop.

02

Wound by your wrist

The rotor winds it as you move. Take it off on Friday evening and it's still running on Sunday morning.

03

Signed on the back

One watchmaker builds each watch from the first screw to the last, then engraves their initials on the caseback.

Inside the Diver

Six layers, front to back.

The same order you saw at the top of the page. Each one is made, finished and checked before the next goes on.
