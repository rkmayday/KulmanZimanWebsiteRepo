Questions

Good to know.

The things people ask before their first detail.

How long does a detail take?

A Wash & Protect takes about two hours. A Full Detail takes most of a day, and Polish & Coat can take one to two days, depending on your paint.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We bring our own water and power, so we can work in your driveway or garage anywhere in our service area.

Is it safe for ceramic-coated or wrapped cars?

Yes. We use pH-neutral soaps, clean microfiber for every panel and no brushes on the paint, so coatings, wraps and PPF stay protected.

How do I get a price?

Tap "Book now", tell us your car and what it needs, and we'll reply with a quote. Prices depend on the car's size and condition.

What if it rains on the day?

We'll move your appointment to the next clear day at no cost, or work under cover if your garage has room.

How often should I get my car detailed?

A full detail every three to four months keeps most cars looking new, with a wash every couple of weeks in between.
