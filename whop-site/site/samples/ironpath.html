FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Our team replies within a day.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Foundation Strength starts with the basics and every movement has a form video.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Tell us your equipment and the plan only uses what you have.

What happens if I miss a session?

The plan moves it or drops it and adjusts the rest of the week, so you never fall behind.

Can I switch programs?

Any time. Your history carries over, so the new program starts at the right weights.

Is the first week really free?

Yes. Cancel any time in the first seven days and you will not be charged.

Does it work with my watch?

Ironpath reads heart rate and sleep from Apple Health and Google Fit to shape your recovery score.
