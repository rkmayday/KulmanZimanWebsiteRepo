Security

Powerful, and on a short leash.

Synthra only touches what you allow, and every action is reviewable after the fact.

  • Scoped permissions. Read and write access per tool, per team.
  • Human approvals. Choose which actions always need a person.
  • SSO and SCIM. Your identity provider stays in charge.
  • Data residency. Keep data in the US or the EU.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Security questionnaires and DPAs are available on request.

Does Synthra train on our data?

No. Your data is used only to complete your tasks and is never used to train models.

What happens if the agent gets something wrong?

Risky actions need approval, and every step is logged and reversible where the tool allows it.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first tools and automate a first workflow in one afternoon.

Can we limit what it can touch?

Yes. Permissions are set per tool, per team and per action.

Do you support single sign-on?

SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning are included on the Enterprise plan.
