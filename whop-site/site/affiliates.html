Affiliate FAQ

Questions about the program.

Do I need experience in web design or sales?
No. You do not build anything or manage any projects. Your job is to introduce businesses that need a website and share your link.
Is this a full-time job?
No. It is a part-time, flexible role with no minimum hours and no schedule.
Am I an employee of Ziman Sites?
No. Affiliates are independent. You are not an employee, partner or agent of Ziman Sites, and you are responsible for your own taxes.
When and how do I get paid?
Commissions and payouts are handled through Whop, where you can see your earnings once you join.
What happens if a customer gets a refund?
Commission is earned on purchases that stay paid. Refunded or charged-back purchases do not earn commission.
Can I buy through my own link?
No. Commission is for new customers you bring to us. Purchases by the affiliate or on their behalf do not qualify.

Affiliates are independent and have no authority to make commitments on our behalf. The full Affiliate Program Terms apply.
